I've sat these exact exams myself — and come out the other side.

I came out of GCSE Maths with a grade 9 and A-Level Maths with an A, and I'm now studying for a BSc in Mathematics and Finance at City St George's, University of London. The exam board's tricks, the question patterns, the bits everyone gets stuck on — I remember all of it, because I went through the same syllabus recently enough that none of it has faded.

I tutor because one-to-one time is where maths really clicks. In a class of thirty there's rarely room to stop on the one step that's actually holding you up. There's a clear path from "I don't get it" to "obvious". My job is to find that path for you specifically — not to run you through generic worksheets.

The first lesson is free, no commitment. If we click, we keep going. If we don't, you've lost an hour and learned something either way.
