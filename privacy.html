Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

SafMaths is a GCSE and A Level maths tutoring service in Tooting, London, run by Mohammed Zayyan Safwan. We look after the details you give us and decide how they are used.

This policy covers information you give us through our website, our Facebook and Instagram ads and lead forms, and WhatsApp.

What we collect and why

We collect only what we need to reply to your enquiry and arrange tutoring.

What we collectWhy we collect it
Your nameTo address you when we reply
Your phone numberTo contact you by WhatsApp or phone about tutoring
Your child's year group (GCSE or A Level)To tell you whether and how we can help
The topic or grade your child is aiming forTo prepare for a first conversation
Messages you send us on WhatsAppTo answer your questions and arrange lessons

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying to people who ask about tutoring, and taking steps at your request before a lesson is agreed. We do not use this information for anything else, and we do not sell it.

Who sees it and how long we keep it

Only SafMaths sees the details you give us. We do not share them with other people or companies, with two exceptions:

  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp) handles the information when you submit a lead form or message us through their services. Their own privacy policy applies to that.
  • When the law requires it, for example if a court or regulator asks.

We keep your details for as long as we are talking to you about tutoring, then for up to 12 months in case you get back in touch. After that we delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop using your information for contact about tutoring

Email us using the address below and we will respond within one month.

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy or your information: mzsafwan@hotmail.com.

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
